# Contributing

Contributions are welcome when they make the framework clearer, safer, easier to apply, or more useful across civic domains.

## Good contributions

- Improve plain-language definitions
- Add a reusable template or fictional example
- Clarify evidence, privacy, quality, governance, or publication practices
- Improve Mermaid diagrams and accessibility
- Correct ambiguity or inconsistent terminology
- Add responsible implementation-neutral guidance

## Please do not contribute

- Credentials, tokens, personal data, or private records
- Production endpoints, infrastructure identifiers, or exploitable topology
- Proprietary application code or database schemas
- Real household locations or individual travel histories
- Claims about a community without traceable evidence and limitations
- Vendor promotion that weakens the technology-neutral approach

## Contribution process

1. Open an issue describing the question or gap.
2. Keep the proposed change focused.
3. Explain the civic and architecture value.
4. Update related templates or diagrams when terminology changes.
5. Confirm that no secret, personal, or product-internal information is included.
6. Submit a pull request with a clear summary and review notes.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution may be distributed under the repository licence.
